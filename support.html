Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Patron purchase on a new device?

Open Settings in Marian, scroll to Marian Patron, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID that made the original purchase.

How do I view my purchase details?

Purchases are managed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name at the top → Purchase History to view your Marian purchase.

You can also tap View Purchase in the Marian app's Settings if available.

I purchased Patron but premium features are still locked.

Try these steps in order:

  • Open Settings → Marian Patron → Restore Purchases
  • Make sure you are signed into the correct Apple ID
  • Force-close the app and reopen it
  • If still locked after 5 minutes, restart your device
  • If the issue persists, email us with your purchase receipt
How do I add the home screen widget?

Long-press an empty area of your home screen → tap the + in the top-left → search for Marian → choose the Weekly or Monthly streak widget → tap Add Widget.

Widgets are a Marian Patron feature. Free users will see a "Marian Patron" placeholder.

My streak isn't updating correctly.

Streaks update when you complete a full Rosary or Divine Mercy Chaplet. Make sure you tap through to the final prayer step.

Patrons can manually mark a day as completed in the expanded calendar (tap the calendar on the home screen, then tap a day to toggle).

Can I pray in a different language?

Yes! Open Settings → Language and choose from 21 supported languages including English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, German, Polish, Latin, Tagalog, and more.

Why am I seeing ads?

Marian no longer includes ads. If you see anything unexpected, please update to the latest version and let us know.

Reminders aren't firing.

Check the following:

  • iOS Settings → Notifications → Marian → make sure Allow Notifications is on
  • In Marian: Settings → Reminders → make sure the toggle is on and a time is set
  • Make sure your device is not in Focus or Do Not Disturb mode at the reminder time
  • Reminders are a Marian Patron feature
How do I change the theme?

Open Settings → Appearance and tap any theme card. Free users have access to the Default and Default Dark themes. Patrons unlock 10 saint-inspired themes.

Does Marian collect my data?

No. All your prayer history, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device and never sent to us. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Will Marian work without an internet connection?

Yes — once installed, all prayer functions work fully offline. An internet connection is only required for App Store purchase verification or to make a Patron purchase.
