Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Marian iOS application ("Marian", "the app", "we", or "our") handles information when you use the app.

In short: Marian does not require an account, does not contain advertising, and does not use tracking or analytics services. Your prayer activity, intentions, settings, and other app data remain stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Marian does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information for advertising, analytics, or tracking purposes.

We do not collect or receive:

  • Your name, email address, or account information
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, or location
  • Your prayer history or completed devotions
  • Your prayer streaks or statistics
  • Your prayer intentions
  • Your selected theme, language, text size, or other preferences
  • Analytics about how you use the app
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking information

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Marian stores information locally on your device so that the app can remember your preferences and prayer activity.

This may include:

  • Prayer completion history and streak information for the Holy Rosary, Divine Mercy Chaplet, and Holy Wounds Chaplet
  • Prayer statistics and progress
  • Prayer intentions you choose to enter
  • Selected theme and language
  • Prayer text size and other app preferences
  • Prayer reminder settings
  • Information needed to display Marian Home Screen widgets
  • Marian Patron access status as provided through Apple StoreKit

This information is stored using local storage provided by iOS and is not sent to Marian servers.

Deleting Marian removes the app's locally stored data from your device. Purchase records associated with your Apple Account are maintained separately by Apple and may be used to restore eligible purchases.

3. Offline Use

Marian's prayer content is designed to work without an internet connection. The Holy Rosary, Divine Mercy Chaplet, Holy Wounds Chaplet, prayer texts, mysteries, and other core prayer content are included within the app.

An internet connection may still be required for services provided by Apple, such as purchasing or restoring Marian Patron through the App Store.

4. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

Marian does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or third-party analytics services.

Marian does not track you across apps or websites.

5. Apple StoreKit and In-App Purchases

Marian Patron is available through in-app purchases processed by Apple using StoreKit.

Marian Patron may be purchased through either a recurring 3-month subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Apple processes payments and manages transaction information associated with your Apple Account. Marian does not receive your credit card number, banking information, or other payment credentials.

The app may receive information from StoreKit indicating whether a valid Marian Patron purchase or subscription is active so that Patron features can be unlocked.

For information about how Apple handles your information, please review Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Notifications and Prayer Reminders

Marian Patron users may choose to enable prayer reminders.

If you enable reminders, Marian requests permission to send notifications through iOS. Reminder schedules are stored on your device and are used to deliver the reminders you configure.

You can change notification permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

7. Prayer Intentions

Marian Patron allows you to enter prayer intentions for use during prayer and with certain reminder features.

Prayer intentions remain stored locally on your device. Marian does not receive, review, transmit, or store your prayer intentions on an external server.

8. Home Screen Widgets

Marian Patron includes optional Home Screen widgets that can display information such as prayer streaks.

Information required by Marian's widgets is stored locally on your device and shared only between the Marian app and its widget extension through Apple's local app storage mechanisms.

9. Children's Privacy

Marian does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or behavioral tracking.

If you believe personal information has been transmitted to us unexpectedly, please contact us using the information below.

10. Data Security

Because Marian's prayer data and preferences remain on your device, their protection depends in part on the security protections provided by iOS and your device.

We recommend keeping iOS up to date and protecting your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID where available.

11. Your Data

Marian does not maintain a user account or remote database containing your prayer history, intentions, statistics, or preferences.

You can remove Marian's locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

Subscriptions and purchase history are managed by Apple through your Apple Account and the App Store.

12. International Users

Marian may be available in multiple countries and languages. We aim to respect applicable privacy laws wherever the app is offered.

Because Marian does not operate user accounts, advertising systems, analytics services, or a remote database containing your prayer activity, Marian generally has very limited access to information about its users.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Marian changes or as legal and platform requirements change.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
