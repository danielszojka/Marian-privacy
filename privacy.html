Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Marian iOS application ("Marian", "the app", "we", "our") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about what data is and is not collected.

Summary: Marian does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and stores all your prayer data locally on your device. The app does not use any advertising services.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Marian does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, or location
  • Your prayer history, streaks, or completed devotions
  • Your selected theme, language, or app settings
  • Any analytics or usage data of your activity within the app

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Prayer streak counts and completion history (Rosary and Divine Mercy)
  • Selected theme, language, prayer text size, and other preferences
  • Reminder schedule (if enabled)
  • In-app purchase status (Patron entitlement)

This data is removed when you delete the app from your device.

3. Third-Party Services

Marian does not use advertising services or ad SDKs. The only third-party service used is Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases.

3.1 Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases)

In-app purchases (Marian Patron, Monthly Patron) are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework. Apple manages payment processing, receipts, and subscription renewals. We do not see, store, or transmit your payment information. Refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Children's Privacy

Marian is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

5. Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, security depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping iOS up to date and using a device passcode.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to disclose, modify, or delete. To remove all locally stored data, simply delete the app from your device.

To manage your in-app subscription, visit Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iOS device.

7. International Users

Marian is available worldwide. Because we do not collect personal data, GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations do not impose data subject rights against us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
